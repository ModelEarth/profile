US State Trade Flow Sankey

Purchases between US states in 2019, from Exiobase's US trade split among states with BEA GDP by state. View the international Sankey.

What this page shows: a state-to-state Sankey of US purchases in 2019, filtered by supplying industry and direction.

Flows are built from trade-us.csv (each Exiobase industry pair's national purchases and factors 901–910), industry_category.csv and category_state.csv (each state's share of US GDP by category, from BEA). A flow from one state to another is the national amount times the supplying state's share times the buying state's share. See bea/notes.md.

Summary cards split the selected state's purchases into in-state, other states and imports, and give its sales to other states. Specializations are categories where the state's share of US GDP is at least 1.25 times its share of all GDP.
